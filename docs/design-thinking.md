# Design Thinking

## Users
- Maintenance staff
- Facility managers
- Sustainability coordinators

## Pain Points
- Alert fatigue from noisy thresholding
- Poor context when incidents occur after-hours
- Limited explainability of anomaly scores

## Design Response
- Building-aware expected baseline
- Multi-method model comparison
- Risk tiers and plain-language explanations
- Evidence retrieval from local guidance notes
- Dashboard-first workflow for operational clarity
