# Impact Estimation & Indicators

AquaGuard AI reports **potential** excess consumption based on deviations from dynamic facility baselines.

## Measurable Indicators:
1. **Potential Excess Water Identified (L)**: Total volume of consumption exceeding expected baseline during anomalous events.
2. **Anomalous Events Detected**: Frequency of incident clusters flagged across campus infrastructure.
3. **High-Risk Response Prioritization**: Percentage of incidents triaged into HIGH tier requiring urgent physical dispatch.
4. **Detection Latency Reduction**: Reduction in mean time to identify unusual consumption patterns (from weeks via utility bills to < 1 hour via telemetry).

## Stakeholder Beneficiaries:
- **Campus Facilities Teams**: Rapid dispatch and targeted fixture checks before small leaks become major ruptures.
- **Sustainability Coordinators**: Empirical tracking toward **UN Sustainable Development Goal 6 (Target 6.4: Water-use efficiency)**.
- **Campus Administrators**: Transparent, audit-ready operational accountability.

## Ethical Framing:
All impact figures represent *simulated potential indicators*, not proven historical savings from deployed hardware. AquaGuard AI strictly avoids fabricated deployment claims.
