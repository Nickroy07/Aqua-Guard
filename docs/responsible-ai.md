# Responsible AI

## Fairness
Building-specific context is used to avoid penalizing larger buildings solely by absolute volume.

## Transparency
The system presents observed vs expected consumption, deviation values, risk drivers, and plain-language explanations.

## Ethics
Anomaly signals do not confirm physical leaks. Human verification is mandatory before maintenance action. Autonomous shutoff is out of scope.

## Privacy
Workflow is designed for aggregate facility data and avoids personal-level identifiers.

## Uncertainty
Risk score indicates relative concern, not physical certainty. Recommendation text uses cautious wording.

## Synthetic Data
This dataset is synthetic and is used for development, demonstration, and evaluation. It does not represent actual campus utility records.
